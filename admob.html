04 · 0-to-1 Monetization UXR Google AdMob UXR · 2014–2017 · Sole UX Researcher

Rebuilding Google AdMob around how app businesses run

Overview: As AdMob's sole UX researcher from 2014 to 2017, I built the research program from zero and led 24 mixed-methods studies across Ad Network Mediation (Mediation Groups) and a Material Design web console redesign for 1M+ mobile apps.
01 · 0-to-1 Research Foundation

Built a 1,267-publisher panel

  • AdMob had operated for two years without dedicated UX research or a participant panel when teams started planning two multi-quarter rewrites.
  • I launched a 3-week homepage banner inside AdMob that opted in 1,267 verified publishers across indie developers and multi-app gaming studios.
02 · Early Roadmap Pivots

Led 24 mixed-methods studies

  • Studio interviews and a 2-day design sprint pivoted ad mediation from static "Shared Stacks" to rule-based Mediation Groups before engineers wrote code.
  • Card sorts, navigation prototypes, and a 107-publisher tree test proved the new app-centric console caused no performance regression.
03 · Google I/O 2017 Impact

Migrated 300,000+ publishers

  • The team launched both overhauls together at Google I/O in May 2017 and cut core monetization workflows to 1 to 2 steps.
  • Leadership built usability and satisfaction targets into launch gates, and our panel and 3-step information architecture (IA) method scaled to Firebase, AdSense, and DoubleClick.
Takeaway Building a 1,267-publisher panel and leading 24 mixed-methods studies redirected two flagship AdMob roadmaps before engineering build and scaled to 300,000+ publishers.
02 / Problem & Stakeholders › Ambiguity & Audiences

Why the right product direction was initially unclear

Context: After two years without dedicated UX research or a participant panel, teams saw large studios choosing rival mediators and new developers dropping off during onboarding. Conflicting internal assumptions made the right fix unclear.
Ad Mediation Ambiguity

Mediation scale vs. price control

  • Ad mediation routes each ad slot through a ranked "waterfall" of ad networks, yet AdMob tied waterfalls 1-to-1 to ad units (ad placements).
  • Studios updated thousands of country price floors (minimum bids) by hand. Teams assumed one static shared waterfall ("Shared Stacks") would scale and missed why studios distrusted auto-bidding.
Web Console Ambiguity

Org-chart tabs vs. app workflows

  • Legacy "Monetize / Promote / Analyze" tabs mirrored Google's internal teams rather than developer tasks.
  • Leaders worried that switching to an app-centric layout would trigger change aversion and slow down experienced publishers.
Cross-Functional Alignment

Who needed user evidence

  • Product managers, UX designers, engineers, and sales specialists needed workflow evidence to prioritize two multi-quarter rewrites.
  • Partner teams across Firebase, AdSense, and DoubleClick needed shared user data to align roadmaps for Director and VP reviews.
Takeaway Without user research, internal assumptions drove both roadmaps. Teams needed empirical evidence on how studios managed ad pricing and daily app workflows.

04 / Ad Mediation Pivots › From Shared Stacks to Mediation Groups

Pivoting ad mediation from static stacks to flexible rules

Early Architecture Pivot: Early interviews with multi-app studios disproved the core assumption behind AdMob's initial mediation proposal before engineers wrote production code. Follow-up usability rounds caught a priority-order misconception shared by all 14 tested publishers.
01 · Architecture Pivot

Replaced "Shared Stacks"

  • Interviewed multi-app studios did not share identical price floors across apps because payouts shifted by audience, format, country, placement, OS, and screen size.
  • I facilitated a 2-day cross-functional design sprint that pivoted the roadmap from static stacks to rule-based Mediation Groups.
02 · Traffic Safeguard

Prevented traffic drops (14 of 14)

  • Across two usability studies, all 14 tested publishers assumed unfilled requests in Group 1 would pass down to Group 2.
  • To prevent unmatched ad requests from going unfilled, we restored a catch-all Default Mediation Group, clarified priority rules, and added guided tours.
03 · Workflow & Pricing

Eliminated custom hacks

  • Studios wired custom SDK events to call one network at multiple price tiers, distrusted "black-box" auto-bidding, and saw repeated Rewarded Video ads (opt-in video for in-app rewards) hurt retention.
  • Engineering responded by shipping multi-call waterfalls (up to 3x), inline floor editing with reference prices, and video frequency caps.
Takeaway Field and usability studies redirected mediation architecture before production coding and protected publisher revenue from unmatched-traffic drops.
05 / Google I/O 2017 Outcomes › Shipped Impact & Org Scale

Shipped Google I/O outcomes and org-wide research impact

Google I/O May 2017 Launch: Google launched the redesigned Material Design AdMob console and Mediation Groups together at Google I/O in May 2017 and migrated 300,000+ publishers. Both the shipped features and the research systems scaled across Google's publisher tools.
Shipped at Google I/O 2017

Shipped product overhaul

  • The team launched rule-based Mediation Groups, up to 3 calls per network, inline country floor editing with reference prices, and Rewarded Video caps.
  • We also shipped unified Mediation and Revenue reports plus app dashboards pairing ad earnings with Firebase retention and average revenue per user (ARPU).
Workflow Efficiency

Shorter core workflows

  • The redesign cut first ad-unit creation from 2 steps to 1, Ad Review and Blocking from 4 steps to 1 to 2, and Payments from 3 steps to 1.
  • It also reduced multi-network price updates from manual per-unit edits to 1 to 2 steps and simplified signup and onboarding checklists.
Scaled UX Governance

Launch standards & org reuse

  • Leadership built SUS targets (> 68.0 industry average) and monthly satisfaction tracking into launch scorecards and started APAC localization audits from our multi-language benchmarks.
  • I shared the 1,267-publisher panel with Analytics and Firebase and coached AdSense and DoubleClick on our 3-step IA method (card sort, navigation prototype, and tree test).
Takeaway The 24-study program cut core workflows to 1 to 2 steps for 300,000+ publishers and established reusable panels, benchmarks, and IA methods across Google.
