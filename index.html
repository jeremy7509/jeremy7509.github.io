How I lead research

Trained in Applied Cognitive & Human Factors Psychology (Ph.D., Clemson) with 12+ years leading UXR at Google.

Fieldwork + statistical modeling

I pair qualitative discovery with choice-based conjoint, MaxDiff, and regression-based driver models so product and engineering leaders can make high-stakes roadmap calls with clear trade-offs.

Empirical AI workflows

I treat AI adoption like a research program: test where models genuinely help, build strict evidence guardrails where they fail, and keep real users at the center.

Scaling craft across teams

I grow mixed-methods researchers and build shared tools, playbooks, and quality standards so product teams move faster without bottlenecking research or lowering rigor.
