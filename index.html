01 / Philosophy & Methodology

How I turn ambiguity into product clarity

Great research doesn't end in a slide deck. I partner closely with product managers, designers, and engineers from early problem framing through post-launch evaluation so insights directly drive product decisions.

By combining the depth of qualitative context (why people behave the way they do) with the scale of quantitative behavioral data (what is happening and how often), I help teams de-risk bets and build with confidence.
