02 / Career Trajectory

14+ Years of Research Leadership

User Experience Research Lead & Manager · Google Messages

Google · Kirkland, WA

May 2019 – Present

Platform Strategy & AI Research

  • OEM Default Strategy & Apple RCS Interoperability: Directed choice-based conjoint research quantifying how preloaded messaging apps influence smartphone OS choice, helping persuade Samsung and global OEMs to make Google Messages their default messaging app worldwide (1B+ MAU). Led mixed-methods studies on Android–iOS messaging friction that informed Directors, VPs, SVPs, Samsung executives, and Google Product Marketing—contributing to Apple adopting RCS in iOS 18.
  • AI Research Tooling & Production Code: Built an autonomous discovery agent used by researchers across Google (turning weeks of multi-source literature and market review into hours), personally shipped live production code joining product usage data with in-product surveys, and created an agentic AI skill that drafts surveys from success criteria and searches the codebase for launch metrics.
  • GenAI & RCS Product Research: Directed foundational and evaluative research for Gemini in Google Messages, Magic Compose, and the global RCS rebrand, clarifying user expectations for conversational AI and messaging identity to guide product design and launch decisions.

People Leadership, Organizational Scaling & Portfolio Governance

  • Team Building & Promotion Advocacy: Built the Google Messages UXR team from a single IC into a 5+ person distributed team by hiring and onboarding 3 FTEs and multiple contract researchers across US sites. Authored individual growth plans and guided 2 direct reports through promotion while maintaining high retention.
  • Portfolio, Budget & Annual Planning: Represent UX Research in Google Messages annual planning with Engineering, Product, and Design Directors and VPs to set multi-year roadmaps, balance capacity between foundational discovery and evaluative studies, protect the team from ad hoc requests, and manage a six-figure annual vendor budget.
  • Developing Mixed-Methods UXRs: Coach embedded researchers across qualitative fieldwork and quantitative modeling (MaxDiff, regression-based key driver analysis) to lead end-to-end roadmap prioritization.

Quantitative Systems, Benchmarking & Field Research

  • Quality Driver & Survey Systems: Lead the international UX benchmarking program using regression-based key driver analysis to identify quality drivers and set org-wide craft OKRs. Partner with Data Science to run the in-product survey program, pairing continuous sentiment tracking (CSAT, product health) with targeted launch intercepts.
  • Global Field Ethnography: Led multi-market studies and moderated field sessions across a 7-country ethnography program (rural low-income US communities, India, Indonesia, the UK, France, Germany, and Sweden), pairing fieldwork with cross-market surveys to shape spam warning dialogs and anti-abuse protections.
